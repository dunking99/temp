Rotation note

Energy led into spring 2025 on a crude squeeze, then lost six ranks in four months as inventories built. Leadership handed off to health care through the summer pause, and since October the market has paid almost exclusively for AI capex — technology has held first for four straight months while utilities quietly climbed as the long end steepened.
